# Morning Brief — topics

The daily run reads this file to decide what news to gather. Add, remove or reorder
sections here (or ask Claude to). Each `##` heading becomes a section on the page.

## Tech
General tech and AI news, big product launches, hardware. Bonus: industrial / networking tech.

## Gaming
New and upcoming game releases, big announcements, notable updates and patches (PC and console).

## BJJ & Grappling
Brazilian jiu-jitsu and submission grappling: ADCC, IBJJF, CJI, Polaris, ONE grappling, notable athletes.

## MMA
UFC and wider MMA: upcoming cards (especially this weekend), results, fight announcements. Flag Irish/UK fighters.

## Motorsport
Formula 1 (results, standings, next race, big stories) and GT racing — GT World Challenge
Europe, 24 Hours of Spa, plus other endurance racing (WEC, Le Mans) when relevant.
